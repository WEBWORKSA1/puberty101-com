Brand-safety standards

Our audience includes young people, so we hold partners to a high bar.

  • All sponsored content is clearly labeled "Sponsored" and editorially independent.
  • No ads or sponsorships for diet products, weight-loss, cosmetic procedures, dating, alcohol, tobacco/vaping, gambling or adult content.
  • No targeting of individual users under 18; no collection of minors' personal data for marketing.
  • Product mentions never replace medical guidance; sponsors can't edit health facts.
  • We reserve the right to decline any partner.
