What great puberty education looks like

Our classroom approach follows widely accepted best practice for health education.

  • Clear ground rules and an anonymous question box
  • Accurate anatomical language
  • Inclusive of every student, including neurodivergent learners
  • Coverage of boys' puberty, not just periods
  • Proactive, transparent communication with families
