Creative challenges that make health learning fun — for classrooms, creators and curious minds. Prizes are funded by supporters and sponsors.

Healthy Me Poster Challenge

Who: Classrooms (grades 4–8)

Students design a poster that teaches one puberty or hygiene fact. Teachers submit the class's best three.

Prize: Classroom resource grant + featured gallery placement

Explain It in 60 Seconds

Who: Creators 16+ (under 18 via parent/guardian)

Make a 60-second, family-friendly explainer on a puberty topic — animation, whiteboard or skit. No faces required.

Prize: Cash prize + paid commission for a full video

Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase or donation necessary to enter or win. Void where prohibited.
  • Entries for anyone under 18 must be submitted by a parent, guardian or teacher. Never include a minor's full name, photo, school or contact details in public submissions.
  • Entries must be original, family-friendly and medically accurate. We may decline any entry.
  • Judging criteria: accuracy (40%), clarity (30%), creativity (30%). Winners are announced on this page and contacted by email.
  • Prize values and deadlines are announced with each round. Prizes are not transferable; winners are responsible for any taxes.
  • By entering you grant Puberty101.com a non-exclusive license to display the entry with credit. You keep ownership.
